TL;DR: Modern monitoring platforms face a “workflow problem”: engineers are drowning in telemetry but lack tools that connect detection to resolution, often leading to fragmented, manual incident investigations. Most organizations have mastered data collection but fail at incident response. Engineers waste precious time manually stitching together logs, metrics, and traces across siloed tools.
